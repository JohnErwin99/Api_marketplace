Pricing questions

Is there a free sandbox?

Yes. Every account gets 1,000 developer sandbox calls a month, shared by everyone on the account. They’re non-production only: sandbox calls get sample data, or Espresso’s test system for enterprise porting, and never reach a live system. They reset on the 1st and are never billed; past the allowance they’re refused, not charged, and we can extend it on request.

When do I need a card?

Only for Standard and Advanced live calls. Free reads and status, service orders and the sandbox need no card. Add one in the API console before your first billable call; the same key keeps working.

What’s billed?

Successful live Standard (1¢) and Advanced (2.5¢) events, invoiced in CAD on the 1st of the next month and charged to your card on the 5th, taxes extra. Failed calls, free reads, sandbox calls and service orders carry no API fee. Telecom services you order are billed under the tariff, at the rates shown before you order, never twice.

What you do
