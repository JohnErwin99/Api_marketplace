Pricing questions

Is there a free sandbox?

Yes. Every account gets 1,000 non-production developer sandbox calls a month. They reset on the 1st and are never billed; past the allowance, sandbox calls are refused, not charged.

When do I need a card?

Only for Standard and Advanced live calls. Free reads and status, service orders and the sandbox need no card. Add one in the API console before your first billable call; the same key keeps working.

What’s billed?

Successful live Standard (1¢) and Advanced (2.5¢) events, invoiced in CAD on the 1st of the next month and charged to your card on the 5th, taxes extra. Failed calls, free reads, sandbox calls and service orders carry no API fee. Telecom services you order are billed under the tariff, at the rates shown before you order, never twice.

Your data and security

Questions, answered

We review every request and you stay in control of your key.

What we do

  • Review every request before any access is granted
  • Keep the keys to Iristel’s systems on our servers, never in your browser
  • Check your approval on every API call
  • Remove access immediately when an account is closed

What you do

  • Keep your API key secret, like a password
  • Never put it in a web page, a ticket or a screenshot
  • Tell us right away if you think it has leaked
