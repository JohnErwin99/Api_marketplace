Your data and security

Questions, answered

We review every request and you stay in control of your key.

What we do

  • Review every request before any access is granted
  • Keep the keys to Iristel’s systems on our servers, never in your browser
  • Check your approval on every API call
  • Remove access immediately when an account is closed

What you do

  • Keep your API key secret, like a password
  • Never put it in a web page, a ticket or a screenshot
  • Tell us right away if you think it has leaked
Who can use the marketplace?

Business customers, Iristel partners, agents and Iristel employees. Choose your path above to see what fits you.

How long does approval take?

Our team reviews every request and emails you as soon as it’s approved.

Do I need to write code?

No. The console lets you try every approved API with simple forms. Your developers can copy the ready-made examples when you want to connect your own app.

What if I need an API that isn’t listed?

After you sign up, choose “Other” on your access request and describe what you need.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The Free package needs no card: once you’re approved you can run health checks, reference-data lookups and status reads straight away. Pick Standard or Premium when you need more, and add a card in the console before your first call.

What does it cost?

There are three packages — Free, Standard and Premium (see Packages & pricing above) — and each includes the one before it. Standard and Premium are one monthly fee with no per-call charges, invoiced on the 1st of each month and charged to your card on the 5th. A call outside your package is never billed: it’s blocked with a message saying which package it needs.

Can I change my package later?

Yes. Ask for an upgrade from the API Marketplace page in the portal. Our team switches your package and the same key keeps working.
